What this is

A web recreation of Glass, an offline DECA Ontario study app for iPhone. It uses the app's real type, colours, motion and question bank.

It is not the iOS build. Haptics, notifications, the widget and on-device AI can't exist in a browser, so they're represented rather than real.

Try

  • Answer a question in Practice — the sounds are the app's own.
  • Move between tabs and watch the sections stagger in.
  • Switch Dark / Light.

Sample questions are original practice items written for this app. They are not official DECA Ontario or DECA Inc. competition content.
