What this is

A faithful web recreation of Glass, an offline DECA Ontario study app for iPhone. It uses the app's real type, colours, motion and question bank — including the same handwriting path that draws the wordmark on launch.

It is not the iOS build. Haptics, notifications, the widget and on-device AI can't exist in a browser, so they're represented rather than real.

Try

  • Flip between iPhone 17 and iPhone 8 — the layout is meant to read identically on both.
  • Answer a question in Practice.
  • Switch Dark / Light.

Sample questions are original practice items written for this app. They are not official DECA Ontario or DECA Inc. competition content.
